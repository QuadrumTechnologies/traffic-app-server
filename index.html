Products

Two product families on one platform. Signal control keeps a junction running safely on its own. Enforcement and surveillance cameras are separate devices. The Traffic Control and Monitoring System connects both, and connects your systems to them.

1 Traffic signal control

Traffic · Hardware

TSLC 2.0 junction controller

Our current controller. A backplane carries a comms card, a power card and one signal card for each approach, up to four, so a fault is cleared on site by swapping a card.

  • Ethernet, Wi-Fi, 433 MHz and 2.4 GHz radio links, RS485, and a GSM option
  • Runs on 9–36 V DC, with a solar-panel charging input
  • Battery-backed real-time clock keeps day plans running if the network drops
  • Inputs for pedestrian push buttons and a police panel (next stage, hold, manual)
  • Hardware watchdog, temperature sensor and fault buzzer
  • Firmware updates over the air (OTA), without opening the cabinet electronics

2 Enforcement and surveillance

Vision · Pilot projects

ANPR and red-light enforcement

Number-plate recognition and red-light violation capture. At a TSLC junction the camera takes the red signal over a wire from the controller, and the evidence records the plate, the time and the lamp actually lit.

Speed and junction cameras

Radar-assisted speed capture where a client needs it, and junction cameras that count and classify traffic for planning and adaptive timing.

Safety stays local.The controller never waits on the network to keep a junction safe, and a camera’s red-signal input is wired, not sent over the internet.

Devices only talk to us.Controllers and cameras connect out to our platform over encrypted links. Nothing at the roadside is reachable from the internet.
